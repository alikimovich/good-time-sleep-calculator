Good Time helps you wake up between sleep cycles instead of in the middle of one, so mornings feel easier.

A night's sleep is made of cycles of about 90 minutes, and a good night has five or six of them. Good Time does the counting for you.

WAKE UP
Going to bed now? See the times to wake up after 1 to 6 full cycles, counted from when you'll fall asleep. The 5-cycle time is marked as recommended.

BED TIME
Pick the time you need to be up and see when to go to bed for 1 to 6 full cycles.

WAKE-UP ALARMS
Tap the alarm button beside any wake-up time to set a real system alarm for it. It rings with the system alert, and you can stop it there. Choose the sound in Settings: the system alarm sound or one of Good Time's own tones (Sunrise, Chime, Pulse), with a preview. Alarms need iOS 26 or later.

MAKE IT YOURS
Set how long it usually takes you to fall asleep and how long your sleep cycles are, and every time updates.

Good Time works on iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode, in English, Spanish and Russian. No account, no ads, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

The 90-minute cycle is an average, and everyone's sleep is a little different. Good Time is a planning aid, not medical advice.
